Services

The right intervention for the decision in front of you.

Three ways of working that can begin with a diagnosis, progress to implementation, or sustain a measurement capability over time.

Clear scope

Each engagement is shaped around the question, the available data, and how your team makes decisions.

01

Marketing Measurement Health Check

A focused assessment of what you can measure today, which gaps matter, and which next step will create the most value.

  • Attribution, reporting, and KPI definition review
  • Data availability, quality, and measurement gap map
  • Incrementality and experimentation opportunities
  • Prioritized roadmap for MMM, forecasting, or optimization
02

Forecasting & Budget Optimization

Models, decision tools, and optimization systems to project value, explore scenarios, and allocate investment under real-world constraints—from recommendations to autonomous execution.

  • LTV, revenue, and ROAS forecasts
  • Cohort economics and investment scenarios
  • Channel and campaign allocation with constraints
  • Online optimization of budgets and ROAS targets, with autonomous execution
03

Fractional Marketing Analytics Leadership

Ongoing guidance that raises decision quality without requiring a permanent leadership structure from day one.

  • Performance reviews and planning
  • Experiment design and evaluation
  • Model monitoring and refreshes
  • Guidance for analysts, marketers, and stakeholders

Capabilities across every engagement

Methods in service of the decision—not the other way around.

01

Marketing mix modeling

Estimates each channel’s contribution to sales and how its returns change at different investment levels.

02

Attribution & incrementality

Combines data from MMPs such as Adjust and AppsFlyer with experiments and other sources to separate attributed conversions from outcomes caused by marketing.

03

Causal inference

Builds a credible comparison to estimate what would have happened without a campaign, change, or intervention.

04

Cohorts & unit economics

Tracks acquisition, retention, and revenue by group to understand CAC, LTV, and payback time.

05

SKAdNetwork & mobile privacy

Turns aggregated, delayed iOS signals into a useful view of performance without individual identifiers.

06

Pipelines & automation

Experience integrating APIs from Google, Meta, AppLovin, TikTok, and other publishers to unify, validate, and refresh marketing data.

07

Model monitoring

Detects performance degradation, data shifts, and outdated assumptions before they affect decisions.

08

Decision tools

Turns results into simulators and dashboards that help teams compare scenarios and act.

A focused first conversation

Start with the decision creating the most uncertainty.

In an introductory conversation, we can frame the problem, review the available evidence, and decide whether there is a useful next step.
