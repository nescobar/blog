Privacy

Privacy, without unnecessary fine print.

This policy describes how the public Data Guasu website currently operates.

Data this site collects

This informational site has no forms, user accounts, or first-party tracking tools. Data Guasu does not collect personal information directly through these pages.

External links

The site links to LinkedIn and may later include a professional email address or scheduling provider. When you follow an external link, that provider’s privacy policy applies.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may process basic technical information—such as IP address, browser type, and security logs—to deliver and protect the site. That processing depends on GitHub Pages infrastructure.

Changes

If analytics, forms, or other data-processing tools are added, this policy will be updated before they are published.
